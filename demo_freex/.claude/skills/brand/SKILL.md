## Voice
- Emit, don't promote. Light and ironic ("ma sei fuori?"), never solemn, spiritual, academic.
- Demo state by design: publish before ready; unfinished is the point.
- Business support for artists, not visibility. Not a promotional platform.
- **Copy belongs to Giordana.** New copy = `[PROPOSAL]`, 2–3 variants. Approved copy (in docs) is used verbatim.
- Ideas discussed ≠ agreements. Never present a hypothesis as decided.

## Instagram
- Captions: **all lowercase, max 2 lines, never hashtags.**
- Pillars: `free.process` · `free.people` · `free.sound` · `free.pieces`
- Phases: silence → frequency (fragments) → material (product, preorder) → event (countdown)
- First post: logo on absolute black + a date, no explanation.
- Bio (preferred, first line to update): "emittiamo. non promuoviamo. / free.quencies ↓"
- Highlights: free.tracks | free.sessions | free.artists | apply | process
- Feed 2–3 posts/week; stories raw and daily. 11-track playlist culminating in free.bumma's track.

## Visual DNA
- Rule: **outside calm, inside everything.** P02 logo + Space Mono = outside. Ink drawing = inside. Never on the same level.
- Three graphic levels: P02 only for the name · Space Mono for information · status indicator as a row of eyelets (`○○●○○○○○○○○` = track 3 of 11).
- Photo: analog B/W, cold, documentary, no filters. Current focus: **clubbing** — 90s/2000s club lights, night, dancing figures, ambiguous spaces, real Zurich places, film/flash, accidental blur, desaturated. No flowers, skies, meadows, generic photos.
- AI images fill gaps, never set the tone.
- **Only existing assets** — see `design-system/inventory.md`. Nothing invented (no new icons, patterns, textures, illustrations, lettering, stock, AI imagery).
