# DEMO_FREEX

Design system of DEMO_FREEX, an Italian cultural label based in Zürich — work in progress, by design. The brand doesn't promote, it emits.

**One rule above all: nothing invented.** Every visual element comes from files made by Marta Cambiaghi (logo P02, Space Mono) or by Giordana (ink drawing, photos). Compose, crop, scale — never draw, generate or recolour. A missing element is a visible `[MISSING: …]` placeholder and a request, never an improvisation.

## Content fundamentals
- Names, exactly: **DEMO_FREEX** (brand) · **freex** (community) · **free.quencies** (system) · `free.track_01` · `free.session_01` · `free.tshirt_01` · `free.bumma`.
- Voice: light and ironic ("ma sei fuori?"), never solemn, spiritual or academic. Short. Unfinished is the point: "Non siamo ancora pronti. È il punto."
- Instagram captions: all lowercase, max two lines, never hashtags.
- Copy belongs to Giordana: proposals are marked `[PROPOSAL]`.
- Never: old names or typos of the brand, "made in" claims, dates not yet confirmed.

## Visual foundations
**Outside calm, inside everything.** The P02 logo and Space Mono are the outside; the ink drawing is the inside. They are never on the same level.

Three graphic levels, never mixed:
1. **Name** — only the P02 logo (assets › Logo). In running text: `DEMO_FREEX` in Space Mono.
2. **Information** — Space Mono, left aligned, separator `——`, sizes from the poster spec (`headline` `info` `body` `small`).
3. **Status** — a row of eleven eyelets, one filled: `○○●○○○○○○○○` = track 3 of 11 (component StatusIndicator).

Colour: `black-absolute`, `ink`, `paper` — nothing else until Marta's palette is delivered and approved. Themes: **Night** (`ground` = black-absolute, `text` = paper, logo _W) and **Paper** (`ground` = paper, `text` = ink, logo _B).

Photography (to come from Marta and Giordana): analog black & white, cold, documentary, no filters; current focus clubbing — 90s/2000s club light, night, dancing figures, real Zürich places, flash, accidental blur, desaturated. No flowers, skies, meadows. AI imagery fills gaps, never sets the tone. Photos enter this system only once Giordana approves them for public use.

## Assets
- **Logo** — P02 by Marta: Line, Stacked, Monogram ("df"), and +Type (text block reads "DEMO FREEX / C 2026": confirmation pending before public use). `_B` = ink, `_W` = paper. Never recolour, stretch, outline or redraw. Clear space and minimum size: not yet defined by Marta.
- **Drawing** — Giordana's ink drawing (scan, low resolution here; originals ≥600 dpi with Giordana for print).

## Formats
post 1080×1350 (3:4) · story 1080×1920 (9:16) · print (A3, t-shirt, stickers): Marta's files only.

## Components
FirstPost (logo on absolute black + one line), StatusIndicator (eyelets), RecCountdown (rec timer `GG:OO:MM:SS`).
